Concept

  • The Cyrillic К doubles as a shield — its diagonals fan open like protecting arms.
  • The amber dot at the joint is a quiet nod to a vaccination mark / completion check.
  • Reads as a strong Latin K too. Arabic readers see a clear geometric mark, not a foreign letter.
StrengthSurvives 60px. Loud and distinctive in the App Store grid.
RiskLetter-first marks can read clinical. Needs warm pairing in app.

Concept

  • A two-leaf sprout: the universal symbol of early growth — pre-verbal, pre-cultural.
  • The two leaves cradle a single amber bud, the only warm color: a hint of the child without depicting one.
  • Zero language load. Reads identically in Moscow, Riyadh, Tashkent.
StrengthWarmest of the three. Calmest in the App Store grid.
RiskSprouts are common in wellness apps — risk of blending in.

Concept

  • Amber header strip echoes the PDF-export button — the icon previews the value prop.
StrengthMost descriptive — clear what the app does from the icon alone.
