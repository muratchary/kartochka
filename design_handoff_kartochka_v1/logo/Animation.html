The seedling, animated.

The mark has a natural growth choreography: stem rises, leaves unfold, bud blossoms. Three variants — a one-shot grow-in for app launch, a calm idle loop for any loading state, and a busy spinner for short waits.

01

Grow-in

App launch. Plays once over ~1.4s, then breathes. Choreographed: bg pops → stem grows up → leaves unfold (l, then r) → amber bud blossoms with a soft overshoot.

1400 ms
02

Idle

Long loads, content fetching, or any waiting state. Leaves sway, bud pulses gently. No motion sickness — peak rotation is 3°.

4200 ms loop
03

Busy

Short waits (network, save). A small dot orbits the bud. Snappier rhythm than idle. Use inline at 24–48 px.

1100 ms loop

How to ship this in the Expo app

All three variants are pure SVG + CSS keyframes. To port to React Native: use react-native-svg for the mark and either react-native-reanimated (recommended) or Animated to drive strokeDashoffset, scale, and rotate on the four named parts. Same timeline values as below.
