## Paste into: App Review Information → Notes

Thank you for the review. Both issues are addressed in this build (1.0, build 24):

GUIDELINE 3.1.2(c) — SUBSCRIPTIONS
• The paywall now shows functional "Terms of Use" and "Privacy Policy" links at
  the bottom of the purchase screen, plus the auto-renewal disclosure. Subscription
  title, length, and price are shown on the plan cards.
  To reach it: open the app → More tab → "Upgrade to Premium" (or tap any
  Premium feature such as the Doctor PDF export).
• The App Store description now includes the Terms of Use (EULA) link.
• Privacy Policy: https://kartochka.app/privacy
• Terms of Use (EULA): https://kartochka.app/terms

GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) — ACCOUNT DELETION
Sign-in is optional in this app (it works fully without an account). When a user
does sign in, in-app account deletion is now available:
  1. More tab → "Sign in / Back up data" → sign in with Apple
  2. More tab → "Delete account"
  3. Confirm in the dialog
This permanently deletes the user's account and all server-side data immediately.

## Reply to send in App Store Connect (Resolution Center message)

Hello, and thank you for the detailed feedback.

We have addressed both items in build 24:

1) Guideline 3.1.2(c): The in-app paywall now includes functional links to our
Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy, along with the auto-renewal disclosure;
subscription name, duration, and price are displayed on the purchase screen. We
have also added the Terms of Use (EULA) link to the App Store description.

2) Guideline 5.1.1(v): We added an in-app "Delete account" option under the More
tab (visible once signed in) that permanently deletes the account and all
associated data. Steps to test are included in the App Review Information notes.

Please let us know if anything else is needed. Thank you.
