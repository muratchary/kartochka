# United Arab Emirates — Childhood Vaccination Schedule (for pediatrician review)

## ما هذا / What this is

هذا مسودة جدول التطعيمات الروتينية للأطفال في دولة الإمارات (من الولادة حتى 6 سنوات) لتطبيق Kartochka. نطلب من طبيب الأطفال التحقق من جميع اللقاحات والجرعات والأعمار قبل الإطلاق.

This is a draft of the routine childhood vaccination schedule for the UAE (ages 0–6) for the Kartochka mobile app.

## المصدر / Source

- Primary: **DHA Clinical Guideline for Best Practice in Immunization**, Code DHA/PHPD/CG-04, **Issue 4, effective 27/01/2026**, Appendix 1 ("National Immunization Schedule for Children Aged 0 Through 6 Years – UAE").
- Mirror: https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/102024/Clinical%20Guideline%20for%20Best%20Practice%20in%20Immunization20241028945.pdf
- Cross-checked: MOHAP National Immunization Programme; DOH Abu Dhabi EPI 2019; DHA Vaccination Guideline 2025 parent booklet.

Drafted: **2026-05-21**.

## يرجى التأكيد / Please confirm

1. **Rotavirus product — RV1 (2 doses) or RV5 (3 doses)?** DHA 2026 Appendix 1 shows 2 doses at 2 and 4 months, but DOH Abu Dhabi (2019) notes RV1 in government clinics and RV5 (3 doses) in private. Which is current in your practice? / أي لقاح روتا يُستخدم — جرعتان أم ثلاث؟
2. **PCV valency.** PCV15 or PCV20? DHA lists "Pneumococcal Conjugate (PCV15 or PCV20)" without specifying which is currently in use. / ما هي صلاحية لقاح المكورات الرئوية — PCV15 أم PCV20؟
3. **MenACWY at 12 months — universal for all children?** DHA 2026 Issue 4 made this universal (vs risk-group only previously). Confirm this is being implemented across UAE. / لقاح المكورات السحائية ACWY عند 12 شهراً — شامل لجميع الأطفال؟
4. **COVID-19 vaccine inclusion.** DHA states "COVID-19 vaccine as per the guidelines" without specifying age/dose for 0–6 — current practice? / ما هي توصيات لقاح كوفيد-19 للأطفال دون 6 سنوات؟
5. **RSV monoclonal antibody (nirsevimab) at birth.** Seasonal universal (1 Sep–1 Mar) for infants whose mother did not receive RSV vaccine in pregnancy. Confirm currently in routine practice. / مضاد RSV عند الولادة (موسمي) — مُطبَّق حالياً؟

## ما لم نُدرجه / What we deliberately left out

Per DHA 2026 Issue 4 Appendix 1, these are NOT in the universal 0–6 schedule (risk-group / catch-up only):

- Hepatitis A / التهاب الكبد A — only in catch-up table (min age 12 mo); not routine.
- PPSV23 — only for high-risk children (asplenia, immunodeficiency, sickle cell, HIV, cochlear implants).
- Nirsevimab beyond birth dose — only for high-risk infants up to 19 months.
- MenACWY additional/accelerated doses — only for complement-deficient patients, travellers to hyperendemic areas, microbiologists.
- HPV — universal but from Grade 8 or age 10-11 (girls); out of v1 0–6 scope.
- Tdap booster at Grade 11.
- Additional COVID-19 doses (not in fixed-age routine).
