Delete your Kartochka account

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Kartochka account and the data associated with it. Kartochka is a child health record app published by Agamyrat Durdymyradov.

Delete your account inside the app (recommended)

If you signed in (Apple, Google, or email) to enable cloud backup or partner sharing, you can delete your account and all of its data directly in the app:

  1. Open Kartochka on your device
  2. Go to the More tab (bottom-right)
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm the action

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our servers — and clears it from your device. The action cannot be undone.

If you never signed in

Kartochka works without an account, and in that case all of your data lives only on your device — we never receive it. To remove it, simply uninstall the app; that erases every child, vaccination, growth measurement, milestone, photo, and note stored locally.

Need help?

If you can't access the app and need us to delete server-side data on your behalf, email support@kartochka.app from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We complete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted: all children, vaccinations, growth measurements, milestones, photos, notes, medical profile data, doctor visits, partner sharing codes, your email, your user ID, and your sign-in history.

What we keep (and for how long): anonymous billing records required by tax authorities (no personal data attached) for the legally required period in your jurisdiction. Apple and Google may also retain transaction records as required by their own platforms.

Subscription cancellation

Deleting your account does NOT automatically cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. To cancel:

  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Kartochka → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kartochka → Cancel

Questions?

Reach us at support@kartochka.app. We typically reply within two business days.
